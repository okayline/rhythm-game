recordings by Edward Loveall (CC0).

## Stable Audio 3 sounds (not CC0)

`house/bed-1.wav` to `house/bed-5.wav` (music beds: pads and bass, no drums) and `house/kick-2.wav` to `house/kick-5.wav`
were generated by kura with Stable Audio 3 (Stability AI; the beds with Small-Music, the kicks with Small-SFX), owned under the Stability AI Community License (free under $1M annual revenue; commercial use
needs a free registration at stability.ai/community-license). Each bed is exactly 15.0 s (8 measures at 128 BPM), cut from a
24 s generation so beat 1 lands on the first count-in hat, with 5 ms fades at both ends.

| File | Prompt | Cut from (s) |
|---|---|---|
| house/bed-1 | "A warm, steady house music loop at 128 BPM with no drums at all…" | 3.760 |
| house/bed-2 | "A minimal, calm house music bed at 128 BPM with no drums and no percussion…" | 0.029 |
| house/bed-3 | same as bed-2 | 0.031 |
| house/bed-4 | same as bed-2 | 0.269 |
| house/bed-5 | same as bed-2 | 0.269 |

The kicks: prompt "A punchy deep house kick drum one-shot with a round, warm low end and a short, tight click on the
attack. Dry, single hit, quick decay." (2 s generations); each hit starts within 0.4 ms of the file's start, cut to 0.3 s
with an 80 ms fade-out, mixed to mono and leveled near kick-1 (peak at most -0.1 dB).
