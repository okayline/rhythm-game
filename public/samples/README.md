## Stable Audio 3 sounds (not CC0)

`tracks/tech-house-1.flac` (the music) and `house/kick-2.wav` to `house/kick-5.wav` were generated by kura with Stable Audio
3 (Stability AI; the track with Small-Music, the kicks with Small-SFX), owned under the Stability AI Community License
(free under $1M annual revenue; commercial use needs a free registration at stability.ai/community-license).

The track: prompt "A modern melodic tech-house track at 128 BPM with a polished, peak-time festival sound. A punchy
four-on-the-floor kick, crisp shuffling hi-hats and a tight clap on beats 2 and 4…" (a 24 s generation). Measured at
128.04 BPM; cut from its first kick (48.2 ms, the median of 64 kick onsets) to exactly 8 measures (661293 samples), with a
5 ms fade at the end, stereo FLAC (lossless, so the start isn't shifted).

The kicks: prompt "A punchy deep house kick drum one-shot with a round, warm low end and a short, tight click on the
attack. Dry, single hit, quick decay." (2 s generations); each hit starts within 0.4 ms of the file's start, cut to 0.3 s
with an 80 ms fade-out, mixed to mono and leveled near kick-1 (peak at most -0.1 dB).
