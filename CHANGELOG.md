## v0.2.0
**Tue Nov 17, 2026 · 11:40 PM HST**

- **A real track:** the music is now a modern tech-house track made with Stable Audio 3 (8 measures at 128 BPM, looped twice), and you tap beat 4 of every measure over it. The day's kit still gives the count-in and the sound of your tap. Removed: the game's own drum patterns and the house beds (Beat Fighter keeps its own).

## v0.1.0
**Tue Nov 17, 2026 · 11:25 PM HST**

- **The first version:** a rhythm game made from Beat Fighter II v0.13.0. Tap beat 4 of every measure of the day's song; each tap is graded perfect, good or miss, with a running count and a final score. The same song as Beat Fighter each day, with its kits, beds and house kicks, the beat lamps, the scrolling lane, calibration and the space background. Removed (from Beat Fighter): the fight (life bars, meters, moves, the ghost, duels, the log), the enemy wind-ups and their cue sound, the ledger page and the hash chain, accounts, runs and save codes, the database, and Beat Fighter's name.
